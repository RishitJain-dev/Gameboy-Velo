# Gameboy-Velo
Gameboy Velo is a modern take on the classic Gameboy, with a joystick instead of a d-pad, and the same A and B buttons as before. This new version includes a large colourful TFT screen and included storage with an SD card reader and even a LiPo battery!

## 1. Designing the schematic - 30 minutes
   I used KiCad to design the schematic



 ## 2. Designing the PCB - 1 hour
   Placed components, routed traces, filled a ground pour and exported STEP and GERBER for later use

 


## 3. Designing the CAD - 45 minutes 
  I used Onshape to design the CAD, splitting the casing into 2 pieces, top and bottom, all connected by friction, with tight tolerances, and button covers, and a rotary encoder dial
